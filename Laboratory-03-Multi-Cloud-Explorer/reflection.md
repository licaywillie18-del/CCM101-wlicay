# Mission Reflection

Among AWS, Azure, and GCP, Microsoft Azure impressed me the most. I chose Azure because it offers more than 200 cloud products and services and provides a wide range of options for computing, storage, databases, security, and artificial intelligence. I also found Azure interesting because of its strong integration with Microsoft technologies such as Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud computing services that allow organizations to use resources without maintaining physical infrastructure. All three platforms provide virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity management, security, and scalable cloud services. They also have global infrastructure that helps organizations improve availability, reliability, and performance.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection. A company needs to consider its budget, existing technologies, workload, scalability requirements, security needs, and geographic location. For example, a company using Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an AI-focused company may prefer GCP because of its strong AI and machine learning capabilities.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that the best choice depends on the specific needs and goals of the organization. I also learned how to compare cloud services and recommend suitable platforms for different business scenarios.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved significantly after completing this mission. I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, compared their services, created client recommendations, and documented my Linux server investigation. This mission helped me organize my work better and gave me practical experience in documenting cloud computing knowledge in GitHub.
